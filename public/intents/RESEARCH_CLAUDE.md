## The Story — Where It Started

On December 15, 1986, Jonie opened the first Dunkin' Donuts store at Fuente Osmeña, Cebu City. He was young. The food industry in Cebu was nothing like it is today. He watched it all grow — the malls, the competitors, the trends — and he moved with it, not against it.

Thirty years later, in 2016, he sat down for an interview to mark the company's anniversary and said:

> "For a person who has been in the industry for 30 years, we have seen the whole stretch and it was exciting."
> "It's no longer a joke to run a food business."
> "You need to be innovative in business. You need to be in the same pace or else you'll get left behind."

That single Fuente Osmeña store became 70+ branches across Cebu, Bohol, Cagayan de Oro, Iligan, Butuan, and Bukidnon. Each region got its own corporate entity. That's not an accident — that's how you build something that lasts.

**Source:** https://www.sunstar.com.ph/more-articles/big-30-dunkin-a-slamming-success

### Yap Fraternity (Cebu), Inc.
- **Role: President, Cebu Chapter**
- Not a college fraternity — a **clan association** for families of the Yeh/Yap lineage, with roots in Fujian, China
- National organization founded: 1946
- Cebu Chapter founded: 1970
- 50th Anniversary of the Cebu chapter: May 2025
- Activities: Chinese New Year dragon and lion dances, disaster relief (fire victims in Brgy. Tejero, outreach in Compostela, Cebu), medical support (partnered with IBP Cebu City Chapter for frontliner food packs during the pandemic)
